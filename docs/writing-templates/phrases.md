# 写作句型库（应用文 + 图表）

来源：用户《背诵手册》「36 个锦囊妙句」第 11～17 页，2026-10-27 拍照录入。
用途：按功能取句，不按编号硬背；只换题干细节。

## 使用原则

1. 先定功能，再选句；不要从第 1 句顺着背。
2. 题目没有的内容不要套；比如没有校长颁奖，就不写 `present awards`。
3. 只借结构，主题词必须换。
4. 长句容易带出低级错误；没把握就拆成短句。
5. 和现有模板重复的，不重复堆。

## 应用文功能句

### 时间、地点、持续时长

```text
The fun sports day will be held at *** on ***, at ***, and it will last for ***.
The library is open from *** on weekdays and from *** on weekends.
```

### 活动目的

```text
This activity aims to inspire *** to ***.
This activity aims to provide *** with a platform to ***.
This activity aims to help *** adapt to college life / foster a deeper understanding of ***.
```

### 活动内容

```text
The event will feature a diverse range of activities, such as *** and ***.
Our president will deliver a welcome address to all participants / present awards to the winners.
```

### 主题、意义

```text
This event focuses on *** / is centered on the theme of ***, reflecting ***.
*** reflects the richness of *** and offers us an opportunity to ***.
```

### 邀请、参与

```text
You'll find it both meaningful and enjoyable if you can join us.
We need *** volunteers, preferably those with relevant experience.
```

### 建议、要求、规则

```text
We kindly request that you observe the relevant rules, ensure your own safety, and dispose of waste properly.
I'd strongly recommend ***.
```

### 附上材料、改善环境

```text
The schedule/details is/are attached for your reference.
We will *** to create a comfortable/quiet learning/work environment for ***.
```

### 祝贺、自豪

```text
I'm so proud of you—this is a wonderful achievement and a well-deserved reward for your hard work.
```

### 补充信件类型

```text
求学/求职：Throughout my undergraduate studies, I developed a strong interest in *** through ***.
投诉：When I opened the package/entered the hotel, I found that *** did not match ***.
辞职：I have gradually realized that *** differs significantly from my initial expectations.
感谢：During my time here, I have greatly valued ***; your support has helped me ***.
```

## Part A 功能补缺（按 17 套真题）

以下功能在 2010～2026 Part A 出现过，优先背这些短句，不要临时拼。

### 感谢 / 祝贺

```text
Thank you so much for ***.
I am truly grateful for your ***.
Congratulations on ***!
I am delighted to hear that ***.
```

### 邀请 / 接受邀请

```text
I am writing to invite you to ***.
I would be delighted if you could join us.
I would be delighted to accept your invitation.
I am writing to confirm that I will attend ***.
```

### 会议/活动细节三件套

题目说“tell him the details”时，至少给：

1. 时间 + 平台/地点；
2. 时长/流程；
3. 需要准备什么或怎么联系。

```text
The meeting will be held on *** at *** and will last about ***.
Please prepare *** before the meeting.
The meeting ID is ***; please join us a few minutes early.
```

### 道歉的具体原因

只说 `My schedule changed` 太虚；题目要求 `explain the situation` 时，要给一个具体原因。

```text
I am sorry to tell you that ***, so I have to cancel ***.
Because of a sudden family emergency, I will not be able to ***.
Now the situation has been resolved, and I will be free to *** next month.
I hope we can meet then.
```

### 道歉 / 投诉 / 要求解决

```text
I am writing to apologize for ***.
Please accept my sincere apologies for ***.
I am writing to complain about ***.
I would appreciate it if you could *** as soon as possible.
```

### 建议 / 征求意见

```text
I would suggest that you ***.
It would be helpful to ***.
I would like to know your opinion on ***.
Please let me know what you think.
```

### 安排 / 介绍 / 感受

```text
I am writing to inform you of ***.
I would like to tell you about ***.
I would like to introduce ***.
I will focus on ***.
I hope we can meet ***.
I would like to put forward a plan for ***.
I have arranged *** for ***.
The event will take place at *** on ***.
I was deeply impressed by ***.
I hope to learn more about ***.
You are warmly welcome to visit ***.
```

## 60 秒取句检查

- 这句话的功能和题目对得上吗？
- 主语、时态、单复数换对了吗？
- 题目没有的数字、人名、机构，是否被带进来了？
- 长句能不能拆成两句，避免语法错误？
- 这句删掉后，段落是否仍然完整？如果删掉不影响，可能只是装饰。
